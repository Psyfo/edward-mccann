# Competitive / Category Positioning

Research conducted 2026-08-14 against live comparator sites (WebFetch of homepages, project pages and practice pages; JS-heavy sites noted where fetches were partial). Comparators chosen for genuine relevance: boutique UK practices, high-end residential with hospitality crossover, strong digital presence. Observation and interpretation are separated; nothing below is copied aesthetics.

## Comparator set (what each does, in one paragraph)

- **McLaren Excell** (mclarenexcell.com): the canonical category formula executed cleanly: hero carousel + one-line positioning statement, Architecture/Interiors split, uniform landscape grid with title + location, captionless image sequences, named senior staff. Designed by Soka Studio. Distinctive for discipline, not invention.
- **Al-Jawad Pike** (aljawadpike.com): the most art-directed site in the set. Numbered project codes ("112 Blackwell Close"), a sortable text **Index** (code/name/location/year/category) beneath the visual grid, numbered carousels, Parma by ABC Dinamo as an identity typeface (identity by Villalba Studio), monochrome palette, luxury clients (Aesop, Celine) beside social housing. A small practice reframed as a rigorous archive.
- **31/44 Architects** (3144architects.com): opens on **Column**, their journal, not the portfolio; projects split into "Selected" (curated masonry grid, mixed aspect ratios) and "Catalogue" (complete archive). Literary project prose citing Enzo Mari and Corbusier; RIBA House of the Year 2024 integrated quietly.
- **William Smalley** (williamsmalley.com): the monograph model: single photograph opening, third-person literary statement ("an architect of rare sensibility"), 18-25 uncaptioned images per project, a "Record" diary with Roman-numeral dating, film photography credited ("Harry Crowder, shot on film"), a Design section selling objects. Authored, personal, reputation-dependent.
- **Proctor & Shaw** (proctorandshaw.com): the engineered case study: poetic overview plus hard data (U-value improvements), full metadata blocks (completed/location/engineer/photographer), awards and press per project, related projects. Listings JS-dependent (a weakness their peers share).
- **Studio McW** (studiomcw.co.uk): the brand-argument model: tagline "Low-tech High Craft", four named principles as an Approach page, one-line editorial summaries on project cards, typology filters, In Progress/Site Updates signalling a living studio.
- **Tuckey Design Studio** (tuckeydesign.com): the editorial-depth model and the closest fit to McCann's mixed portfolio: every grid entry carries title + sector + location + one-sentence description; case studies integrate **captioned** photography, construction shots, sketches, plans; collaborators (including builders) credited; houses, a Berlin hotel and a wine bar presented as one sensibility via sector labels.
- Sampled thinner: O'Sullivan Skoufoglou (tidy Squarespace baseline), Whittaker Parsons (kinetic manifesto tagline, sustainability positioning), Emil Eve (conversion-oriented, testimonial-forward), DGN Studio (one-sentence text homepage, numbered text list), Groupwork (neglected site; "Just another WordPress site" live in markup), Erbar Mattes and Oliver Leech (JS-opaque to fetching; noted, not analysed).

## Category conventions (the entry ticket, 2026)

- Nav: Projects / Studio-or-Practice / Journal-or-News / Contact (+ Instagram).
- Opening: full-bleed image + one-sentence statement, or a straight grid, or (rarer) text-led.
- Portfolio: thumbnail grid with title + location; typology filters increasingly standard; a curated-vs-complete two-tier archive is the emerging boutique trope.
- Case study: hero, 150-300 words, metadata block, captionless sequence, awards/press, next project.
- Voice: first-person-plural craft vocabulary ("considered", "crafted", "quiet", "enduring") doing heavy lifting.
- Look: near-white monochrome grounds, photography carries all colour; no strong comparator runs a dark ground.
- Build: custom WordPress by named small studios, or Squarespace; commissioned identity + site together is the pattern behind every strong example (Villalba/AJP, Soka/McLaren Excell, A Common Purpose/Studio McW).

## Overused patterns (avoid-by-default list)

The poetic one-liner in the "We make X that Y" mould; "award-winning" as the first adjective; uniform grids of interchangeable two-word house names; captionless 20-image dumps credited to the same photographer pool the whole category shares; counter-carousels ("1/20"); starved journals; JS-fragile listings.

## Where Edward McCann currently sits

**Observed**: conceptually on the O'Sullivan Skoufoglou baseline (grid + about + contact, credentials as logos) but executed a generation behind it, minus Instagram, minus even the baseline's tidiness. Simultaneously, McCann's *content* (literate prose, mixed typologies, art collaborations, TV coverage) matches or exceeds several comparators whose *presentation* far outclasses his.

## Differentiation opportunities (grounded in observed gaps)

1. **Editorial depth**: only Tuckey captions images and shows drawings/process. McCann's archive already contains drawings, models and construction imagery, and the writing skill is proven. Out-publishing the set is achievable with existing material.
2. **The two-tier archive at 27 projects**: AJP/31/44 prove the pattern; 27 items is exactly the scale where "Selected + complete Index (with real metadata)" converts modest volume into editorial discipline.
3. **Mixed-typology storytelling**: Tuckey alone frames houses + hospitality as one sensibility; McCann's wine bars, sculpture, furniture and houses are raw material for the same move, currently presented as noise.
4. **Authored first-person voice**: only Smalley occupies it; for an eponymous practice with McCann's writing ability, largely unclaimed territory.
5. **Place literacy**: no comparator builds identity around its own patch; an East London practice with Hackney/Islington/Camden work (plus the Cape Town axis) could own place-based storytelling.
6. **A dark or dark-capable ground**: literally unoccupied among strong comparators; McCann's existing black theme-color and charred-timber material story make it credible (cost: colour-managed photography).
7. **Fast, crawlable, server-rendered delivery**: several admired peers are JS-opaque; technical quality is itself a differentiator here.

## Visual territories the evidence supports (feeds `recommended-design-direction.md`)

- **The Catalogue** (AJP/31/44 lineage): systematised caps wordmark, numbered index, curated grid, one rigorous typeface family, monochrome.
- **The Notebook** (Tuckey lineage): captioned editorial case studies, drawings beside photographs, sector labels unifying the mixed portfolio, warmer serif register.
- **The Monograph** (Smalley lineage): image-led, literary, first-person; highest character, highest writing commitment.
- Territories to avoid: the Squarespace baseline (where the site conceptually already is), the corporate-conversion register (Emil Eve), and the sustainability-manifesto position (occupied and evidence-backed by Whittaker Parsons; McCann's evidence base for that claim is thinner).
