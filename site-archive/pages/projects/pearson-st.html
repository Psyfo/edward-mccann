Pearson St

A conversion of a Grade II listed shop into a residence. The basement beneath the shop was dropped to provide living space which opens to a south facing sunken garden to the rear, while at ground floor what was the shop is converted to the bedroom and bathroom. The stairs down to the basement are situated at the front of the plan beneath one of the shop windows to bring light down to the deep plan of the basement. The stair was pre cut by CNC and assembled on site.
