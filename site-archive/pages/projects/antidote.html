Antidote

Refit of a specialist wine bar and restaurant off Carnaby street. A more efficient plan was developed providing spaces for two distinct types of dining. The ground floor has bar and tapas type eating with high tables and stools allowing greater numbers and a busier feel. The first floor is for fine dining. The fitted furniture is fabricated in steel and plywood with timber and marble counter surfaces.
