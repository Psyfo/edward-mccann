Archway Road

Redevelopment and extension of a factory space already occupied as a residence. The proposals add an additional floor for bedrooms, with living space on the ground floor. A music studio is enclosed in the center of the ground floor. The space and light experienced within the existing structure are retained with double height clerestory spaces and an open plan layout at ground floor.
